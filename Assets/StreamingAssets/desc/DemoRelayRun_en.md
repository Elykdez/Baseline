# Relay Run

Carry the signal to a relay station 600 metres away. Your robot rex runs automatically, gaining speed along the route. Jump over the abandoned equipment and keep going until the relay is reached.

## Controls

- Space, Up Arrow or W: jump. The on-screen Jump button works with mouse or touch.
- P, Escape or Pause: pause and resume an active attempt.
- Retry, or Space after the result conversation: start a fresh attempt.
- Menu: return to the main menu. Leaving an unfinished attempt asks for confirmation.

## Timing your jump

Press jump shortly before the obstacle reaches the rex. You can jump only from the ground; there is no double jump. A press just before landing is remembered briefly, ready for the next jump. Holding a key does not automatically jump again.

The rex's tail is not part of its collision shape. A collision ends the attempt, but you can retry immediately after the guide's response.

## Your mission

Reach 600 metres to complete the delivery quest. Cleared obstacles also count toward the secondary objective. Your best distance and quest outcomes are saved automatically. Leaving before the attempt ends does not count as a completed or failed delivery.

The guide introduces the mission and responds to success or failure. Conversations stop gameplay input, so you can read at your own pace. Losing application focus pauses the run.

## Presentation

Move the pointer across the main menu to see the poster layers shift at different depths. Music, effects and language use the shared settings menu. Relay Run is an example game built with Baseline's definitions, quests, dialogue, alerts, localization, audio and effects systems.
