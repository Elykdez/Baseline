Relay Run demo artwork

Created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this project.
Character style reference: Assets/Bundles/Core/Texture/Characters/RobotGuide.png.
The supplied running reference informed the alternating stance/recovery poses.
No external art pack was imported.

Final assets and prompt specifications:

DemoRelayLandscape.png
Wide illustrated abandoned orbital relay station at dawn; enormous distant
relay ring, layered mesas, sparse towers and cyan signal beams. Quiet darker
left half for menu typography. Smooth hand-inked cel shading, cream, sage,
charcoal, cyan and amber. No characters, foreground objects or text.

DemoRelayForeground.png
Transparent foreground strip of broken cream/sage machinery, charcoal rocks,
sparse grasses and a cyan beacon. Side-on low metal platform, empty upper
canvas. Smooth cel shading, no sky, text, characters or ground shadows.

DemoRelayObstacles.png
Transparent two-cell atlas: broken cargo battery crate and wrecked power-pylon
base. Cream ceramic armor, sage panels, charcoal base, amber cables and cyan
indicators. Separated complete silhouettes, no ground or captions.

DemoRexRun.png
Static robot rex illustration in two cells. Right-facing tyrannosaur with
cream armor, sage panels, charcoal mechanics, tiny forearms, long tail,
cyan eye and amber accents. Used for the icon and dialogue art.
It is NOT used as the gameplay animation.

DemoRexLookout.png
Transparent full-body robot rex in a calm standing lookout pose. Three-quarter
rear view with both feet planted, back and rear hips visible, head raised
toward the distant relay at upper right, segmented tail sweeping down-left.
Cream ceramic armor, sage panels, charcoal joints, cyan optics and amber cables.
Smooth inked cel shading, warm dawn rim light from upper-left. No landscape,
text, floor, shadow blob or duplicate figure. Uses the original rex design and
the user's marked menu screenshot as references. Separate lower-right poster layer.

DemoRexBody.png
Animation puppet body cutout matching the approved rex. Head, torso, long
leftward tail and tiny arms only; remove both hind legs below the hip sockets.
Exposed round hip joint for articulated legs. True alpha, no ground or text.

DemoRexLegParts.png
Three separate neutral rig pieces: upper thigh with hip and knee sockets,
lower shin with knee and ankle sockets, and right-facing three-clawed foot.
Cream/sage armor with dark circular joints and amber details. True alpha.

Import and animation:
Original generated PNGs are retained without repainting or resampling.
Unity imports alpha-cropped parts as Sprite subassets with bilinear filtering
and no mipmaps. The authored prefab has a rigid cutout hierarchy and native
Animator clips. Independent near/far legs use a half-cycle phase offset and
articulated thigh/knee/ankle joints. Run and tucked jump are Animator states;
the run speed follows gameplay speed and freezes during pause/dialogue.
The layered poster uses the existing UIParallax component.

DemoRexLookout.png:
User-selected attachment of the built-in OpenAI ImageGen lookout bust.
Left-facing side-rear view, cream/sage armor, dark joints, amber cables and
a teal eye, with warm sunset light from the left. The supplied transparent
PNG is retained byte-for-byte and enlarged in the lower-right foreground.

Audio:
DemoFootstep.wav is an original 0.12-second mono PCM foot-contact sound at 22050 Hz.
It combines a decaying 145 Hz downward-pitched sine and a short seeded noise transient,
with a 2 ms attack and 15 ms end taper. The cue uses the effects bus at 0.25 gain.
Original PCM WAV masters were synthesized for this project, with authored
sound, music and pooled particle definitions. No external recordings were used.
The retained ambient track is shared content at
Assets/Bundles/Core/Sound/Music/relay_ambient.wav, owned by the Core group
through the Core Addressables folder entry.

DemoRexDust.png:
Generated with the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool. Prompt: single reusable
transparent game VFX sprite for a side-view illustrated robot dinosaur runner;
one low, wide sand-colored dust puff with four soft billowing lobes, warm pale
beige highlights and muted taupe lower shading. Smooth hand-painted shading,
readable at 60 pixels wide, fluffy scalloped silhouette, subtle alpha edge.
No ground, cast shadow, characters, sparks, text, border or checkerboard.
The generated PNG is unchanged. Unity crops the alpha bounds in sprite metadata,
imports at maximum 256 pixels with no mipmaps, and uses the sprite in the
DemoRexFootFx canvas prefab for running contacts, takeoff and landing bursts.
